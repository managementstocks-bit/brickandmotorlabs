Shipping & Delivery

How to Order

Click the price on any kit to reach our contact page, or email us at brickandmotorlabs@gmail.com. Tell us which kit(s) you want and your shipping address. We will review your order, calculate shipping costs, and send you payment details via email.

Shipping Costs

Shipping costs vary by location and order size. We will provide a shipping quote before you pay.

Delivery Times

  • Major cities (Toronto, Vancouver, Montreal, Calgary): 3-5 business days
  • Other urban areas: 5-7 business days
  • Rural & remote areas: 7-14 business days

Returns & Exchanges

If your kit arrives damaged or with missing pieces, contact us within 7 days of delivery and we will send replacement parts or arrange a full return at no cost to you.

For change-of-mind returns, unused kits may be returned within 14 days of delivery for a full refund, minus shipping costs. Kits must be in original, resalable condition.

International Shipping

We currently only ship within Canada. If you are outside Canada, please reach out and we will explore options for you.

Questions?

Have a question about your order? Contact us and we will get back to you within 24 hours.
