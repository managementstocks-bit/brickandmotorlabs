Forklift Power

Age 8+ 180+ Parts $76.68 CAD

Forklift Power is a motorized STEM construction kit inspired by the machines that keep warehouses and factories moving. As your kids build and operate the working lifting mechanism, they discover how motors convert electrical energy into motion to perform real-world tasks. Along the way, they develop problem-solving, logical thinking, and hands-on building skills while bringing an industrial machine to life.
