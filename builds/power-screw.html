Power Screw

Age 8+ 200+ Parts $80.93 CAD

More than just a robot kit, this motorized STEM construction set lets kids build 7 working models, including a dumper truck, scissor lift, pressing machine, and more. As they build and experiment, they discover how real machines use mechanisms like power screws to convert rotational motion into lifting and pushing movements. With versatile parts that support open-ended building, the learning continues long after the included models are complete.
