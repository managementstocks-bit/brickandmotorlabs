Discovering Motions

Age 8+ 176 Parts $134.90 CAD

Some concepts are easier to understand when you can see them in action. Blix Discovering Motions gives your kids the opportunity to build working models that demonstrate how real-world mechanisms function. By assembling different mechanisms and observing their behavior, they develop an intuitive understanding of key scientific principles and mechanical systems. The result is an engaging experience that combines exploration, experimentation, and the satisfaction of making ideas come to life.

What You'll Learn

Discovering Motions introduces kids to types of motions, simple mechanisms, gear systems, gears, and pulleys. Some concepts are easier to understand when you can see them in action.
