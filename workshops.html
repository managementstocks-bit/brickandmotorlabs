We bring the workshop to your classroom

A BrickAndMotorLabs build session runs in your space: we arrive with kits, parts, tools and every instruction manual, and every child builds with their hands. No laptops, no tablets, no screens — just gears, cams, linkages, motors and the moment a mechanism finally moves.

What a session looks like

One mechanism, one build, every child holding the result.

Classroom session

90 minutes, up to 24 students

Build teams of 4, one kit per team. We introduce the mechanism, the teams build it, then we test, tweak and race what they made. Teachers get the explanation, students get the hands.

Camp & club

Build days and week programs

A kit per day or a build that carries across a week, so campers finish something they can prove works. Good for day camps, after-school clubs, library and community centre programs.

Family & community event

A build table at your event

We run a table at fairs, school nights and family events: short builds, short queues, everybody leaves with something they built. Ask about a take-home kit table too.

What the kids actually learn

Every kit is built around a real mechanism, not a model glued together.

  • Gears and gear ratioWhy a small gear turning a big one trades speed for strength.
  • Cams and followersHow a turning circle becomes up-and-down motion, the same idea behind a Ferris wheel drive.
  • Linkages and motionHow levers and linkages steer, lift and push, visible in our crawler and forklift builds.
  • Drive trains and motorsWhere power comes from and how it reaches the wheel.
  • Structure and loadWhy a frame holds, why it twists, and what to change when it does.
  • Testing and fixingThe engineering habit: try it, see what fails, change one thing, try again.

Questions schools ask us

How many students can a workshop handle?

A build team is a maximum of 4 students per kit, so a 90 minute session works well for up to 24 students in one classroom. Bigger groups run as two sessions back to back.

What ages do you work with?

Our kits start at age 3. Classroom and camp sessions are the best fit for ages 5 to 14, and we match the kit to the age group so nobody is handed something they cannot finish.

Do you bring everything?

Yes. Every kit, part, tool and instruction manual comes with us. You provide the room, the tables and the students. Nothing in a session needs a screen, a login or Wi-Fi.

How far do you travel?

Ottawa and the surrounding region in Eastern Ontario: schools, day camps, library programs and community centres. Send your location and we will say plainly what works and what does not.

Can students keep what they build?

Yes, as an add-on: one kit per build team, priced at the kit price shown on this site. It is the option most schools choose for a take-home build day.

Is this connected to the kits you sell?

Exactly the same Blix kits we sell, so a school that likes a session can order the kits afterwards for their own STEM corner. Kit prices run from $12.78.
